Business Loan

AVANI LOAN SERVICES assists proprietors, traders, MSMEs, and self-employed professionals across Latur and Maharashtra with Business Loan advisory and coordination. We help organize financial documentation and coordinate applications with suitable partner banks and NBFCs for term loans, working capital, and equipment financing.

Typical Documentation Overview

  • Business KYC & registrations (Udyam, GST, Shop Act, Partnership/Company deeds)
  • Last 2–3 years ITRs, audited/CA-certified financials, and 12 months business bank statements

Advisory Notice: Final facility limits, pricing, collateral norms, and sanction decisions rest solely with the lending institution.
